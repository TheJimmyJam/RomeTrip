Corfu · Venetian Old Town
📍 Greece · 9 AM – 5 PM
⛅Loading…
Pulling live forecast for Corfu…

Day Plan · Easy Pace

  • 9:00 AM dock; cruise shuttle (~€5) or taxi (~€10) into the Old Town — 3 km, not walkable.
  • Coffee on the Liston promenade — the arcaded café row built under Napoleonic occupation. Sit, watch, slow down.
  • Saint Spyridon Church — Corfu's patron saint, striking red-domed bell tower. Free, two minutes inside.
  • Old Fortress grounds and lower terraces. Walk the entrance; skip the climb to the top — view from the lower level is just as good.
  • Wander the Campiello — the medieval residential lanes barely wide enough for a Vespa. 30 minutes of slow.
  • Long lunch back on the Liston. Be at the shuttle by 4:00 PM.

Where to Eat

Lunch
Pomo d'Oro
Quiet square just off the Liston. Order pastitsada — the Corfiot Venetian-style beef stew over bucatini. The local Sunday meal, on a Monday.
Alt
Salto Wine Bar
Rooftop overlooking the Old Fortress. Cold local rosé, Greek cheese board. The kind of long lunch the cousins remember.

Kotor · The Bay & Walled City
📍 Montenegro · 9 AM – 5 PM
⛅Loading…
Pulling live forecast for Kotor…

Day Plan · Easy Pace

  • Be on deck before 9 AM — the ship spends an hour sailing through the Bay of Kotor. Mountains on both sides. Coffee in hand.
  • Walk off the ship — 5-minute flat walk to the Sea Gate of the walled city. No transport needed.
  • Square of Arms — the main piazza inside the gate, with the 1602 clock tower.
  • Cathedral of Saint Tryphon (1166) — €3.50 entry. Most beautiful interior in town.
  • Saint Luke's Square — Catholic and Orthodox churches side by side, quiet.
  • Maritime Museum in an 18th-c. palace — €5, 45 min, worth it.
  • Skip the wall climb (1,350 steps, no shade). View from the lower terraces is plenty.

Where to Eat

Lunch
Galion
Just outside the south wall, on the marina with a view of the bay and the ship. Whole grilled fish from the daily catch, local seafood risotto. Long, slow.
Coffee
Square of Arms cafés
Best espresso in town. After noon, switch to a Nikšićko (the local beer) and watch the cats — Kotor has a famous resident cat population.

Day Plan · Easy Pace

  • 9:00 AM dock at Gruž, 3 km from the Old Town. Taxi €10 or city bus #1A/1B for €2 to the Pile Gate.
  • Cable car up Mt. Srđ first — €27 round trip, 3 minutes up, no climbing. Best photo of the trip.
  • Stradun — the polished marble main street. Flat, atmospheric, runs the length of the Old Town.
  • Rector's Palace — gothic-renaissance courtyard, cool and quiet. €10, one floor.
  • Franciscan Monastery — has one of Europe's oldest pharmacies (opened 1317). Free to look.
  • Skip the city walls walk — 2 km of stairs, no shade, €35. The cable car gives the same view.

Where to Eat

Split · Diocletian's Palace
📍 Croatia · 9 AM – 5 PM
⛅Loading…
Pulling live forecast for Split…

Day Plan · Easy Pace

  • 500 m flat walk from the cruise terminal along the Riva to Diocletian's Palace. No transport. No hills.
  • Cellars (Substructures) — original Roman undercroft of Diocletian's living quarters. Cool, shaded, atmospheric. €7.
  • Peristyle — the colonnaded ceremonial courtyard. There's a black sphinx Diocletian brought from Egypt c. 1700 BC.
  • Cathedral of Saint Domnius — Diocletian's own mausoleum, repurposed. €5 cathedral floor only. Skip the bell tower (183 narrow steps).
  • Vestibule — round domed antechamber. Listen for klapa (Dalmatian a-cappella) groups singing inside.
  • Coffee on the Peristyle steps (Lvxor café). You're paying to sit on a Roman emperor's courtyard. Worth it once.

Where to Eat

Lunch
Konoba Matejuška
Tiny old fishermen's tavern just outside the palace, by the small harbor. Grilled octopus salad, bottle of Pošip white. Honest, salty, perfect.
Coffee
Lvxor (in the Peristyle)
Cushions on the Roman steps. Order an espresso. Stay for an hour.

Naples · Pizza, Pompeii & Vesuvius
📍 Italy · 9 AM – 5 PM
⛅Loading…
Pulling live forecast for Naples…

Day Plan · Pick One

  • Option A · Pompeii (history pick). Book a private guide — half-day tour with hotel-quality transport (Walks of Italy, Take Walks, Tours by Locals). Cruise-bus excursions are too rushed for an easy pace.
  • Option B · Stay in Naples (the food pick). Cruise terminal opens onto Piazza del Municipio — everything below is walking distance.
  • Castel Nuovo (5 min walk) — 13th-c. fortress at the harbor with Renaissance arch.
  • Galleria Umberto I — a glass-roofed 19th-c. shopping arcade, free, gorgeous, flat.
  • Spaccanapoli — the dead-straight medieval street that splits Naples in half. Walk it slowly; espresso every few blocks.
  • Don't wing Pompeii without a guide — 170 acres, no shade, overwhelming alone.

Where to Eat · Pizza Capital

Lunch

Open since 1870. Two pizzas on the menu (Margherita, Marinara). Take a number. Wait. Eat. The original.

Sweet

1860 belle-époque café on Piazza del Plebiscito. Sfogliatella + espresso at the bar — €1.50 standing, €5 sitting.

Practical Notebook

A reference page worth saving to the home screen.

💶 Money

  • Every port uses the Euro (€).
  • Card almost everywhere. Carry €100–150 cash for cafés, taxis, pizzerias.
  • No-FX-fee card (Capital One, Chase Sapphire, Amex Platinum).
  • ATMs in port towns work fine — avoid cruise-terminal ATMs (worst rates).
  • Tip 5–10% at sit-down meals. Round up at cafés.

📱 Connectivity

  • International plan (T-Mobile, Verizon day-pass) is simplest.
  • Or eSIM via Airalo for Europe — ~$20 for the cruise.
  • Carnival's Premium WiFi tier works for messaging + email on board.
  • Download Google Maps offline maps for each port before you sail.
  • Carnival Hub app is free on board — chat with cousins, see daily program.

🧳 What to Pack

  • Late-Oct Med: 60–72°F days, 50s at night.
  • Layers: tee, sweater, packable rain shell, scarf for breezy decks.
  • One pair of broken-in walking shoes — cobblestones everywhere.
  • One nice outfit for Cruise Elegant nights.
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses, water bottle, day backpack.
  • Universal adapter (Type C/F European plug).

🕓 Time & DST

  • Italy / Croatia / Montenegro: CET (UTC+1).
  • Greece (Corfu): EET (UTC+2) — 1 hr ahead.
  • EU DST ends Oct 25, 2026 — clocks fall back overnight (the night you sail from Italy toward Greece). Carnival adjusts ship time and posts in the daily program.
  • Rome to Dallas: 7-hour difference most of the year.

Cousins Crew · Traditions

Small rituals that turn a 9-night cruise into a story you tell for years.

One Long Table

Cousin dinner every single night — same table, no peeling off. Photo at dessert.

Best-Moment Round

End every dinner with a go-around: best moment of the day. No repeats. Specifics only.

The Cabin Door

Decorate one cousin's door each port day — printed photo from that day, taped up by morning.

The Group Pose

Same cousin pose at every port, in front of the obvious landmark. Five identical shots = one beautiful printed grid back home.

The Halloween Costume

Oct 31 is Naples / Halloween onboard. Coordinate one accessory — black turtlenecks, paper masks, anything. Show up.

The Final Toast

Last night, last drink, on deck, just the cousins. "To Rome, the cousins, and the next one." Phones away two minutes.
